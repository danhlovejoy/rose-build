Deliverable 1: Final Portfolio Presentation

Overview

You design your own project that demonstrates at least four Course Learning Outcomes from this course. On Tuesday, May 5, we'll use the Assignment Sheet Generator to finalize your custom assignment sheet and rubric in class. You'll select your CLOs, describe your project, and download a document that becomes your contract for the presentation. Then on Tuesday, May 12 (5:30 PM), you present for five to seven minutes.

What to Cover

  1. The problem and your approach (2–3 minutes): What you built, what problem it solves, and why you chose the techniques you did. Show your application running.
  2. CLO demonstration (4–5 minutes): Walk through how your project demonstrates each of the CLOs you selected. Point to specific code, outputs, and results. This is the core of the presentation.
  3. Scope and reflection (2 minutes): What worked, what you'd do differently, and whether you used your contingency plan. Be honest about what you cut and why.

Format

You can walk through a Colab notebook, demo a Gradio app on Hugging Face Spaces, or run your code in any other environment — as long as the audience sees your application execute. I'll ask follow-up questions after each presentation.

What to Submit

  • Your code goes in your GitHub repo. Submit the repo link in Canvas.
  • Your GitHub repo should be complete and clean: all lab notebooks in their week folders, README updated, no exposed secrets.

100 points possible. Each criterion is scored on a 4-level scale (Full Credit → No Credit). Your custom assignment sheet will include rubric descriptions tailored to your specific project.
