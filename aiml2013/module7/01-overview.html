What's in This Module

  1. Portfolio Prep Guide: How to choose your projects, structure your narrative, build your visual aids, and practice your delivery.
  2. Combined Portfolio: Multimodal Portfolio. For dual-enrolled students presenting a single project covering both courses.
  3. Assignment Sheet: Portfolio presentation details, CLO list, proposal format, and due dates.
  4. Final Reflection: Prompts, rubric, and submission details for the end-of-semester reflection (due May 14).
  5. Deployment Guide (optional): How to convert a Colab notebook into a Hugging Face Spaces web app. Includes a worked example (Art Style Explorer) and step-by-step instructions for pre-computing embeddings so your app runs on the free tier without a GPU.
Tip: The portfolio presentation is 10% of your grade — the single largest individual assignment. Don't treat it as an afterthought. The students who do well are the ones who rehearse at least twice, time themselves, and cut material until they're comfortably within their window.
